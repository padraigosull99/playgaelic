How to Play

GaelGrid is GAA noughts & crosses. Every square sits where a county (down the side) meets a trait (across the top).

  • On your turn, tap an empty square and name a footballer who fits both — e.g. a Kerry man who won an All-Star.
  • Right answer → the square is yours. Wrong answer → your turn ends and the square stays open.
  • Each player can only be used once in a grid.
  • First to three in a row — across, down or diagonally — wins. Board fills with no line? Most squares wins.

Players come from 60 years of Gaelic football legends. Play the computer or pass the phone to a mate.

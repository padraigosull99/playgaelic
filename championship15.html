How to Play

Championship 15: draft a fantasy fifteen, then take them through a full Gaelic football season. The perfect season is 13-0-0 — wins, draws, losses.

1 · The Draft

The reel lands on a county team and era — legends or underdogs. Pick one player; he can only play in his real position line. You get one county swap, one era swap, and difficulty-based re-spins.

2 · The League

7 group games against Division 1's finest — Dublin, Kerry, Armagh, Donegal and co. Win = 2pts, draw = 1. Finish top 2 to reach the League Final. League slip-ups don't end your season — but they end perfection.

3 · The Championship

Provincial semi-final and final on your chosen route, then the All-Ireland quarter-final, semi-final and final. Knockout games can't be drawn — extra time decides them. Lose in your province and you fight on through the All-Ireland series; lose an All-Ireland knockout and the season is over.

4 · The Prize

Three trophies: the National League, your province, and Sam Maguire. You can win Sam with losses on your record. You can win the treble imperfectly. But 13-0-0? That's immortality.

Drafting Tips

  • Mind the spine. Goalkeeper, full-back, centre-back, midfield and the central forwards carry extra weight — and a strong spine earns a chemistry bonus.
  • Balance beats glamour. Your weakest line drags the whole rating down. Six forwards can't save four corner-backs from Laois.
  • Chemistry counts. Stacking players from one county or one era nudges your rating up.
  • The league is a minefield. Seven games against Division 1 opposition — even great teams drop points. A draw kills 13-0-0 but not your season.
  • Take the keeper when he comes. One slot, few elite keepers. If Cluxton appears, you take him.
  • 13-0-0 is meant to be near-impossible. Even an elite fifteen goes perfect roughly once in fifty seasons. Winning Sam is the job; perfection is the legend.

How to Play

Championship 15 is a draft-and-simulate challenge built on a database of Gaelic football's greatest (and pluckiest) county teams.

1 · The Spin

Each pick, the reel lands on a county team and era — it might be Kerry's Golden Years (★★★), it might be Laois 2003 (★). Better teams mean better players, so the spin matters.

2 · The Pick

Choose one player from that panel. He fills the next open slot in his line — goalkeeper, the 6 backs, midfield, or the 6 forwards. Once a line is full, players from that line stop appearing.

3 · Lifelines

You get one county swap, one era swap, and a difficulty-based number of full re-spins. No take-backs on picks.

4 · The Championship

You picked a province — win its quarter-final, semi-final and final. Champions go straight to the All-Ireland semi-final, then the final in Croke Park. Every game plays out live, score by score. Five games, five wins. One defeat ends the run.

Drafting Tips

  • Mind the spine. Goalkeeper, full-back, centre-back, midfield and full-forward carry the heaviest weight in the simulation. A weak spine loses semi-finals.
  • Balance beats glamour. Six superstar forwards and a shaky defence will be found out in Croke Park. Your weakest line drags the whole rating down.
  • Respect the underdog spin. Land on a ★ team and there's still value — their best one or two players (a Matt Connor, a Dessie Dolan) hold their own anywhere.
  • Every road runs through a final. Whatever province you pick, the early rounds are winnable — it's the provincial final and the business end in Croke Park that find you out.
  • Take the keeper when he comes. There's only one goalkeeper slot and few elite keepers in the pool. If Cluxton appears, you take him.
  • Even a perfect 15 isn't safe. The best possible team goes 5–0 less than half the time. That's the point.

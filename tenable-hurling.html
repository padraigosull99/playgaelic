How to Play

Tenable is all about lists of 10. Each day there's one football list and one hurling list — the same for everyone.

  • Type answers one at a time. Get one right and it locks into the board.
  • Spelling is forgiving — nicknames and small typos still count.
  • A wrong answer (or one not on the list) costs a strike. Three strikes ends it.
  • Get all 10 for a clean sweep, then share your result.
  • No clock — take your time. Come back tomorrow for a new list.

Want more? Hit Practice for a random list any time.

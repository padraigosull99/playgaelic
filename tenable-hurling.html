How to Play

Tenable is all about lists. Each round shows one question — and the hidden list of answers that go with it.

  • Type answers one at a time. Get one right and it locks into the board.
  • Spelling is forgiving — nicknames and small typos still count.
  • A wrong answer (or one that's not on the list) costs a strike. Three strikes and the round is over.
  • Fill the whole list for a full-house bonus.
  • Beat the clock — each list is timed.

Five lists per game. Rack up the highest score you can.
